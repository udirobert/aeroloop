# Storylines — AeroLoop submission video (picked: C)

## C — "Run 11" (PICKED)
Device: result first, inverted. Form: match cuts.
Opens on a score going DOWN — 55.1 in red — in a challenge literally called
"gets better every time it runs". The film then explains the failure: the
×1.20 rule that peaked at 87.03 on 8 tasks, breaking on 24 unseen ones; the
agent's own diagnosis; the repaired playbook; 63.65. Closes on the git-mode
proof (the agent reads its skill from a cloned repo at a pinned commit) and
"the agent found its own overfitting". The honest-failure hook is the
differentiator no competitor can copy.
First 2s: a score readout flipping 87.03 → 55.1, red "run 11" stamp.
Beats: hook (drop) → what it is → the rule that won → the break → the agent
fixes itself → recovery → git-mode proof → end card.
Look: session-log medium — dark ruled paper, JetBrains Mono, phosphor cyan
accent, red reserved for failure.
Sound: low synth drone + sub pulse, VO (ElevenLabs v4, George), hits at the
stamp and the recovery.

## A — "The scoreboard"
Device: a number lands. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
summary.csv is the stage: run rows light up, score column climbs, agent-commit
hashes pulse. The hard suite drops and one row turns red; the frame rebuilds
around the diff that fixed it. Terminal aesthetic, data-forward.

## B — "The ghost writes back"
Device: character does the work. Form: flight through one world.
The spectral city from ghost-film.mp4 is the world; error wisps rise off the
monitors, camera dives into a wisp → becomes SKILL.md rewriting itself. Most
cinematic, softest data proof. (ghost-film.mp4 still used in scene 2.)
